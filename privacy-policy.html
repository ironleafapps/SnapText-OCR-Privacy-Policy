Privacy practices

Retro Street Games is provided by Ironleaf Studio. For privacy questions, email ironleafapps@gmail.com.

Information and services

  • The app can run offline. Gameplay progress and settings are stored on your device. If you sign in to Google Play Games, the app uses your Play Games player profile for Play Games features.
  • When available and enabled, the app submits scores and achievements to Google Play Games Services and can save supported progress using Play Games Saved Games. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms and account controls.
  • Scores submitted to a Play Games leaderboard may be visible to other players with the player name associated with your Play Games profile.
  • The app does not currently connect to a Supabase backend or create a separate Retro Street Games account. Older releases may have used online services; contact us if you have a question about data from an earlier version.

Service providers

Google Play Games Services provides sign-in, leaderboards, achievements, and Saved Games. Google AdMob provides ads, and Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent where required. See Google's Privacy Policy and Supabase's Privacy Policy for information about their services, including data from older app versions if applicable.

Advertising

The app may show Google AdMob interstitial ads at breaks after games. You may also choose to watch a rewarded ad to unlock a country; this is optional, and countries can still be unlocked through play. Debug builds use test ads.

Every ad request is configured for child-directed treatment and a maximum ad content rating of G. Advertising services may still process information such as IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to deliver ads, measure performance, and prevent abuse. See how Google uses information from partner apps.

Before requesting ads, the app checks consent status using Google's User Messaging Platform. Where a privacy choices form is required, you can open it from the app.

Intended audience

The app is intended for players age 13 or older. Offline play is available without signing in. Google Play Games features require a Google Play Games profile.

Retention and security

Local progress remains on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Google Play Games data is managed through your Google Account and Google's retention settings. For information about older-version data or a privacy request, email ironleafapps@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page shows when this policy was last revised.

Delete game data

This app does not currently create a separate Retro Street Games account or provide an in-app account deletion flow. To delete Play Games data associated with this game, use Google's Play Games data deletion instructions. Deleting Play Games data may remove progress, achievements, or leaderboard information and does not delete data held separately by a game developer.

To erase locally stored app progress, open Android Settings → Apps → Retro Street Games → Storage → Clear storage. For questions about data from an earlier app version, email ironleafapps@gmail.com. Never send your password or sign-in codes.
